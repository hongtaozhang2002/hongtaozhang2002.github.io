Ph.D. Student

Hongtao Zhang.

I'm a Ph.D. student at the School of Advanced Interdisciplinary Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). My research centers on Large Language Model Pretraining.

My main interests are Training Dynamics and Optimization Theory. I study the spectral structure and stability of large-scale training, and use theoretical insights to understand and design more effective optimizers.

I am drawn to the quiet possibility that countless small updates can grow into understanding, and to the search for simple principles beneath all that computation.
